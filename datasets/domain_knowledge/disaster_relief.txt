# Disaster Relief Operational Protocols

## Core Objective
The primary objective in a disaster relief scenario is rapid assessment and resource distribution in unpredictable, partially destroyed environments. Time is critical, and safety hazards are abundant.

## Hazards
- Structural collapses and unstable debris.
- Flooding and waterlogged areas.
- Downed power lines and electrical hazards.
- Secondary structural failures (aftershocks).

## Agent Roles
- Aerial Reconnaissance (UAV_Quad): Maps the disaster zone rapidly from above, identifies passable routes through rubble.
- Heavy Payload Delivery (UAV_Heavy): Drops critical medical supplies and communication relays into isolated zones.
- Ground Pathfinding (UGV_Scout): Navigates dense rubble, identifies structural weaknesses, and marks safe paths for heavier vehicles.
- Supply Transport (UGV_Carrier): Moves bulk supplies (food, water, heavy rescue equipment) along ground routes cleared by the UGV_Scout.

## Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
1. Launch UAV_Quad for high-altitude mapping.
2. Deploy UGV_Scout to test ground stability on identified routes.
3. Once routes are cleared, dispatch UGV_Carrier with bulk supplies.
4. For completely isolated zones, deploy UAV_Heavy for precision drops.
