# Logistics Operational Protocols

## Core Objective
The primary objective in a warehouse logistics scenario is high-throughput, efficient item retrieval and transportation in a highly structured but dynamic environment.

## Hazards
- High-density traffic (other robots).
- Narrow aisles and blocked paths from dropped items.
- Dynamic human presence in mixed zones.
- Wi-Fi dead zones deep within metal shelving.

## Agent Roles
- Inventory Scanning (UAV_Quad): Flies above the shelves to scan barcodes and update the central inventory database in real-time.
- Heavy Payload Lifting (UAV_Heavy): Not typically used in indoor logistics due to rotor wash and size, but can be used for high-bay heavy lifts if necessary.
- Rapid Courier (UGV_Scout): Fast-moving ground robot used for single-item express delivery across the warehouse floor.
- Pallet Transport (UGV_Carrier): Heavy-duty automated guided vehicle (AGV) used to move entire pallets of goods from receiving docks to storage aisles.

## Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
1. UAV_Quad conducts periodic sweeps to maintain inventory accuracy.
2. Incoming orders trigger the dispatch of UGV_Carrier for pallet-level movement.
3. Express or high-priority single items trigger UGV_Scout dispatch.
4. Agents must adhere strictly to predefined traffic lanes to prevent deadlocks in narrow aisles.
